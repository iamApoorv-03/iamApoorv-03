### 🔍 Research Focus

My core interest is **trustworthy and fair medical AI** — understanding 
how Vision Transformers and Foundation Models encode anatomical biases, 
and designing architectural interventions that resolve subgroup performance 
disparities without sacrificing clinical accuracy.

Current work spans:
- **Algorithmic Fairness in Oncological Imaging:** Investigating subgroup disparities and architectural interventions in Vision Transformers. Currently analyzing the failure modes of standard mitigation strategies (e.g., adversarial debiasing, importance reweighting) to develop more robust and equitable diagnostic pipelines.
- **Biomedical Signal & Image Processing:** AI/ML for biosignal analysis 
  and biomedical imaging at IIT Hyderabad.
- **Causal & Privacy-Preserving Frameworks:** Multimodal oncology 
  stratification under federated constraints.
